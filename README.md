# CropLend Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Executive Summary & Product Vision

* **Product Name:** **CropLend** (`croplend`)
* **Tagline:** *The Hardware-Agnostic Credit & Compliance Middleware for Precision Agriculture*
* **Product Positioning:**
* Independent B2B Ag-FinTech middleware specifically designed for **open-field precision agriculture, embedded finance (Arvest Bank, regional lenders), and ESG compliance**.
* Fully separated from **VeriAgri** (which also targets open-field precision agriculture, but remains focused on the multi-vendor data hub and audit-ready ESG reporting; CropLend owns the credit & lending layer).


* **Core Value Proposition:**
* Unifies multi-vendor hardware silos (John Deere, Netafim, Sentek) into a single, normalized JSON Schema.
* Replaces rigid, real-estate-collateralized loans with **real-time data-driven credit scoring and automated crop lien valuation**.
* Eliminates greenwashing and supply chain de-listing risks by serving as an unalterable **Chain of Custody** pipeline for ESG standards (CDP Water, TNFD, Scope 3).

## 2. Target Personas & User Scenarios

```
 [Open-Field Row-Crop Farmer]           [Bank Underwriter / Copilot]           [ESG Auditor / Supply Chain]
  ├── Multi-vendor hardware              ├── Dynamic Credit Score (300-850)     ├── Chain of Custody Proof
  ├── Auto-unit normalization            ├── Recommended Limit (USD)            ├── Scope 3 Carbon Emissions
  └── Pay-after-Harvest Financing        └── Real-time Risk Assessment          └── CDP Water / TNFD Reports

```

### ① Open-Field Row-Crop Farmer (Farm Manager)

* **Needs:** Seamlessly aggregate soil moisture, irrigation, and machinery telemetry across heterogeneous hardware without vendor lock-in. Utilize real-time yield and water efficiency data as collateral to secure working capital loans (Pay-after-Harvest) during input purchasing.

### ② Commercial Bank Underwriter (e.g., Arvest Bank Officer / Copilot Assistant)

* **Needs:** Move beyond historical tax returns and physical land appraisals by leveraging real-time field health, water efficiency, and equipment utilization to issue instant, dynamic credit lines at lower default risk.

### ③ Corporate SCM / ESG Auditor (Enterprise Procurement & Verifier)

* **Needs:** Verify that farm-level sustainability claims (water saved, low-carbon cultivation) are cryptographically validated at the edge and instantly export certified audit reports (CDP Water, TNFD, Scope 3 GHG Protocol).

## 3. System Architecture & Requirements

```
 [Field Hardware Layer]
  ├── John Deere (ISOBUS / Machinery & Fuel) ──┐
  ├── Netafim (VRI / Drip Irrigation)          ├──> [1. CropLend Abstraction Layer (OSS Connectors)]
  └── Sentek (Drill & Drop / Soil IoT)        ──┘     - Normalized to `CropLend Unified JSON Schema`
                                                      - Edge SHA-256 Hashing & Cryptographic Signatures
                                                             │
                                                             ▼
                                                [2. CropLend Core Engine]
                                                      - Dynamic Credit Scoring (300-850)
                                                      - Anomaly Detection (Physics-data cross-validation)
                                                      - Scope 3 & Water Efficiency Delta Calculation
                                                             │
                                                             ▼
                                                [3. CropLend Integration & Delivery Layer]
                                                      - CropLend MCP Server (Claude Desktop & M365 Copilot)
                                                      - Embedded Finance API (Checkout / Working Capital)
                                                      - Audit-Ready Exporters (CDP, TNFD, Scope 3)

```

### ① Hardware-Agnostic Abstraction Layer (OSS Connectors)

* **John Deere Connector:** Extracts ISOBUS/CAN-bus fuel metrics, covered hectares, engine hours, and converts imperial units (gallons, acres) to metric standards (liters, hectares).
* **Netafim Connector:** Ingests Variable Rate Irrigation (VRI) flow rates ($m^3/h$), total water applied, and fertigation dosing ($N, P, K$ in kg).
* **Sentek Connector:** Parses multi-depth soil moisture (% VWC), Electrical Conductivity (EC in dS/m), and soil temperature across $10\text{ cm}, 30\text{ cm}, 60\text{ cm}, \text{and } 100\text{ cm}$ probes.

### ② Chain of Custody & Anomaly Detection

* **Hardware Cryptographic Proof:** Verifies SHA-256 hashes generated by edge sensor hardware (Secure Element) alongside ECDSA signatures to prevent manual data tampering.
* **Physics-Based Cross-Validation:** Automatically flags discrepancies between pump power consumption, reported water flow, and volumetric soil water changes.

### ③ Dynamic Credit Scoring & Embedded Finance Engine

* **Dynamic Credit Score ($300 - 850$):** Continuously calculates farm creditworthiness by weighing real-time water efficiency delta (vs. regional baseline), soil water retention, machine productivity, and historical yield data.
* **Crop Lien Valuation:** Evaluates crop asset value to recommend real-time credit limits ($\text{USD}$) for instant working capital pre-approval.

### ④ Model Context Protocol (MCP) & Exporters

* **CropLend MCP Tools:** Exposes `get_farm_telemetry`, `evaluate_credit_score`, `verify_chain_of_custody`, and `generate_audit_report`.
* **One-Click Audit Exporters:** Generates audit-ready evidence packages matching CDP Water, TNFD, Scope 3 GHG Protocol, and GRI Standards.
